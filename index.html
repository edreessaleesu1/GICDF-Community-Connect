Who We Are

A Community-Led Vision for Lasting Progress

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) is a community-focused initiative committed to supporting inclusive development in Gangara, Makarfi Local Government Area, Kaduna State, and surrounding communities.

We believe that lasting progress begins when people participate in identifying their needs, shaping solutions, and working together to achieve shared goals.

Through community engagement, skills development, education, partnerships, and responsible leadership, we seek to help build a future in which more people have the opportunity to thrive.
