Who We Are

Development begins when communities work together.

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) is a community-focused initiative committed to improving lives and expanding opportunities for people in Gangara, Makarfi Local Government Area, Kaduna State, and neighbouring communities.

We believe lasting progress is built through collaboration, responsible leadership, education, practical skills, community participation, and partnerships that respond to real local needs.

Our approach is non-partisan and inclusive. We seek constructive engagement with community members, traditional institutions, public bodies, civil society organisations, and development partners.

  • Community-led and needs-based development
  • Equal opportunities for young people and vulnerable groups
  • Transparency, accountability, and responsible leadership
  • Partnerships that support long-term progress

Our Purpose

To mobilise people, knowledge, resources, and partnerships towards stronger communities where individuals can learn, contribute, live with dignity, and access meaningful opportunities.

Our Vision Thriving, peaceful, inclusive, and self-reliant communities where everyone has an opportunity to contribute and progress.
Our Mission To support community development through youth empowerment, education, skills development, civic participation, environmental responsibility, and strategic partnerships.
Our Commitment To listen to communities, encourage practical solutions, use resources responsibly, and promote cooperation for sustainable development.
