Who We Are

A Community-Led Vision for Lasting Progress

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) is a community-focused organisation working towards inclusive development in Gangara, Makarfi Local Government Area, Kaduna State, and surrounding communities.

We believe lasting progress begins when people participate in identifying their needs, shaping solutions, and working together to achieve shared goals.

Through education, skills development, community engagement, partnerships, and responsible leadership, we seek to help create opportunities for more people to thrive.

How We Work
