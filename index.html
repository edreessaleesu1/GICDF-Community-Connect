About GICDF

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation is a community-focused initiative committed to inclusive development and expanding opportunities for people in Gangara and surrounding communities in Makarfi LGA, Kaduna State.

Our Mission

To support sustainable development through youth empowerment, education, skills, livelihoods, health awareness and community participation.

Our Vision

Inclusive, peaceful and self-reliant communities where everyone has the opportunity to contribute and thrive.

Our Approach

Listening to community needs, working with partners, encouraging volunteerism and promoting transparency and accountability.

Our Development Priorities

We aim to connect community members with programmes, practical skills and opportunities that support long-term development.
