Who We Are

Local commitment.
Lasting community progress.

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) is a community-focused organisation based in Gangara, Makarfi Local Government Area, Kaduna State, Nigeria.

We promote inclusive development through youth empowerment, education, skills development, community participation, peacebuilding and sustainable opportunities.

Our approach recognises that lasting progress begins when communities participate in identifying their needs and shaping practical solutions.
