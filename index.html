Who We Are

Local commitment.
Lasting community impact.

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) is a community-focused organisation working to promote inclusive development and sustainable opportunities.

Our priorities include youth empowerment, education, community development, peacebuilding, and stronger community participation.
