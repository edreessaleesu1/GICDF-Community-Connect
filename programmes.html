Our Priorities

Where We Aim to Make a Difference

GICDF's programme priorities are designed to respond to community needs and encourage inclusive participation. Activities will depend on identified needs, available resources and suitable partnerships.

Youth Empowerment and Skills Development

Promote access to practical skills, digital literacy, entrepreneurship, vocational training, career guidance and opportunities for young people.

Intended outcome: Young people better equipped to pursue livelihoods and contribute to community development.

Education and Learning Support

Encourage educational participation, learning support, mentorship, literacy, digital learning and collaboration with relevant educational stakeholders.

Intended outcome: Improved access to learning opportunities and stronger educational engagement.

Community Development and Welfare

Support community needs assessments, local development initiatives, social welfare activities and constructive engagement around essential community services.

Intended outcome: Communities better able to identify priorities and pursue practical solutions.

Peacebuilding and Social Cohesion

Encourage dialogue, mutual understanding, peaceful conflict resolution, community cooperation and positive relationships among residents.

Intended outcome: Stronger trust, cooperation and peaceful community relations.

Health and Environmental Sustainability

Promote health awareness, environmental education, sanitation, responsible waste management and community participation in environmental protection.

Intended outcome: Greater awareness and participation in healthier, cleaner environments.

Leadership and Civic Participation

Encourage responsible leadership, civic awareness, volunteerism, community dialogue and transparent, inclusive decision-making.

Intended outcome: More informed participation and stronger accountability in community affairs.

Women and Vulnerable Groups

Encourage inclusive development opportunities for women, persons with disabilities and other groups who may face barriers to participation.

Intended outcome: More inclusive access to community opportunities and support.

Community Partnerships and Resource Mobilisation

Build relationships with civil society organisations, public institutions, responsible businesses, donors and volunteers to support shared development priorities.

Intended outcome: Stronger cooperation and improved access to relevant expertise and resources.

From Ideas to Action

How Programme Activities Are Developed

01. Identify Needs

Listen to residents and identify priorities through appropriate community engagement.

02. Plan Responsibly

Develop realistic objectives, activities, budgets, timelines and monitoring measures.

03. Seek Collaboration

Identify suitable partners, funding opportunities, volunteers and technical support.

04. Monitor and Learn

Review progress, document activities and use lessons learned to improve future work.
