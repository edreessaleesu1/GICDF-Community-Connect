Our Story

Community Development Begins with People

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) is a community-focused initiative based in Gangara, Makarfi Local Government Area, Kaduna State, Nigeria.

We are committed to supporting inclusive community development by encouraging young people, strengthening local participation and promoting practical responses to the needs and aspirations of our communities.

Our development priorities include education, youth empowerment, skills acquisition, community welfare, environmental responsibility, peacebuilding and accountable leadership. We aim to work constructively with community members, institutions, civil society organisations and development partners to identify needs and pursue sustainable solutions.

We believe lasting progress is achieved when communities participate in shaping their future and when opportunities are accessible to people regardless of background.

Our Vision

To contribute to peaceful, inclusive and thriving communities where young people and families can access opportunities and participate meaningfully in development.

Our Mission

To promote community-led development through youth empowerment, education, skills development, social welfare, civic participation and constructive partnerships.

Our Purpose

To help connect community needs with ideas, people, resources and partnerships that can support practical and sustainable progress.
