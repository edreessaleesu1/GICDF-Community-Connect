Responsible Communication

News you can approach with confidence

Community information is most useful when it is clear, accurate and shared responsibly. We aim to provide updates that help people understand our work and identify relevant opportunities.

Always verify time-sensitive opportunities with the organisation or the original issuing body before submitting personal information or making payments.

  • Announcements should be checked before publication.
  • Proposed initiatives should not be presented as completed projects.
  • Partnerships should only be described as confirmed when verified.
  • Opportunity details should include the original source where available.
  • Personal information should be shared only through appropriate channels.
