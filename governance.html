Governance and Accountability

Building public confidence through responsible leadership and transparent practices.

Our Principles

Responsible Community Leadership

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) recognises that public trust is essential to effective community development. We aim to promote responsible leadership, ethical conduct, transparency and accountability in our organisational activities.

Our governance arrangements should support decisions that reflect the organisation's purpose, applicable legal requirements and the interests of the communities we seek to serve.

This page outlines our governance commitments. Formal governance structures, policies and procedures should be read alongside the organisation's approved constitution and applicable registration documents.

Our Commitments

Governance Standards We Aim to Uphold

Transparency

Communicate relevant organisational information clearly and provide appropriate reporting on activities and use of resources.

Accountability

Encourage clear responsibilities, documented decisions and appropriate oversight of programmes and organisational affairs.

Integrity and Ethics

Promote honest conduct, responsible decision-making and respect for the dignity and rights of others.

Financial Responsibility

Encourage proper authorisation, accurate recordkeeping, budget oversight and responsible management of funds and assets.

Inclusion and Fairness

Promote fair treatment, respectful participation and decisions consistent with the organisation's objectives and policies.

Legal and Policy Compliance

Seek to comply with applicable laws, registration obligations, approved policies and relevant donor requirements.

Organisational Responsibility

Governance in Practice

Effective governance requires more than statements of principle. It depends on appropriate structures, documented procedures and consistent implementation.

Leadership and Oversight

Roles and decision-making responsibilities should be defined in the approved constitution and relevant governance documents.

Financial Records

Income, expenditure, supporting documents and financial approvals should be recorded and reviewed appropriately.

Programme Monitoring

Activities should have clear objectives, appropriate monitoring arrangements and honest reporting of progress and challenges.

Safeguarding and Complaints

Appropriate procedures should be developed to address concerns, protect participants and handle complaints responsibly.
