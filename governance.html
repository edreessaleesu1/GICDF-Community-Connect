Governance and Accountability

Strong community development requires responsible leadership, clear decision-making, transparent processes and respect for the people an organisation exists to serve.

Our Principles
Responsible Leadership

Building trust through good governance

Gangara Integrated Community Development Foundation (GICDF) aims to promote community development through responsible, inclusive and accountable organisational practices.

Our governance approach should support the proper use of resources, responsible conduct, meaningful participation and decisions that align with the organisation's approved objectives and governing documents.

Governance must be more than words

Governance arrangements, appointments, policies and reporting practices should reflect GICDF's actual approved documents and implemented procedures. This page describes guiding principles; it is not a substitute for official legal or financial records.

Our Guiding Principles

The values that should guide our decisions

These principles provide a foundation for responsible organisational conduct and community engagement.

🔎

Transparency

Communicate clearly about organisational priorities, decisions and activities, while protecting confidential and personal information.

⚖️

Accountability

Encourage responsible conduct, appropriate oversight, accurate records and explanations for important decisions.

🤝

Integrity

Promote honesty, ethical conduct, responsible stewardship and respect for the organisation's mission.

🌍

Inclusion

Encourage fair participation and respectful engagement across different groups within the community.

📋

Responsible Management

Support careful planning, proper documentation, appropriate authorisation and responsible use of resources.

🛡️

Safeguarding and Respect

Seek to protect the dignity, privacy and safety of community members, volunteers and other participants.

Responsible Stewardship

Accountability in decisions and resource management

Community trust depends on responsible handling of money, property, information and other resources entrusted to an organisation.

GICDF should maintain appropriate financial and administrative records and use documented authorisation procedures consistent with its governing documents, applicable legal requirements and any funding agreements.

Reporting and review arrangements should be proportionate to the organisation's activities and obligations.

  • Keep accurate records of income, expenditure and supporting documents.
  • Use appropriate approval procedures for financial transactions.
  • Document important meetings, decisions and assigned responsibilities.
  • Identify and manage conflicts of interest appropriately.
  • Protect organisational records and personal information.
  • Meet applicable reporting, filing and regulatory obligations.
  • Review activities and learn from feedback and identified shortcomings.
