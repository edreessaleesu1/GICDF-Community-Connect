Community Progress Starts With Participation

Sustainable community development is a shared responsibility. GICDF welcomes expressions of interest from people who want to contribute positively to Gangara, Makarfi LGA and surrounding communities.

Whether you are a student, skilled professional, volunteer, community leader, organisation or concerned resident, your knowledge and ideas may help us identify better ways to address local needs.
