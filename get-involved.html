Working Responsibly

Participation should be meaningful, respectful, and transparent.

To build trust and make the best use of people's contributions, GICDF encourages clear communication and responsible participation.

✓
Respect the community

Treat community members with dignity and listen to different perspectives.

✓
Be honest about your contribution

Communicate your skills, availability, and capacity clearly.

✓
Follow agreed responsibilities

Understand the activity, expectations, and relevant safeguarding requirements before taking part.

✓
Protect trust and transparency

Do not make unauthorised promises, represent unconfirmed partnerships as official, or collect funds on behalf of GICDF without formal authorisation.

Before you get started

Tell us a little about yourself, your interests, the skills or support you may be able to offer, and the kind of community issue you care about.

GICDF can then consider whether your proposal aligns with its objectives and current capacity. Contacting us does not automatically establish a volunteer role, partnership, funding commitment, or project approval.

Important: Please use GICDF's official contact channels. Do not send sensitive personal documents or money to an individual claiming to represent the organisation without independently verifying their authority.
Getting Started

Four simple steps

Begin with a conversation and explore a practical way to contribute.

01

Introduce Yourself

Share your name, location if relevant, interests, and the reason you want to connect.

02

Explain Your Interest

Tell us whether you are interested in volunteering, skills sharing, community ideas, or organisational collaboration.

03

Discuss the Possibilities

Explore whether there is a suitable activity or a practical next step to consider.

04

Agree on Next Steps

If there is a suitable opportunity, clarify responsibilities, expectations, and timing before proceeding.
